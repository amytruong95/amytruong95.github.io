Welcome to the Family

PLUR Collective is a community for people who love electronic music and the culture around it. Whether you have been to a hundred shows or are getting ready for your first one, you belong here. We bring ravers together through meetups, kandi-making nights, and group trips to events, all guided by the four values of PLUR.

Join the Collective

What PLUR Means

Peace

We keep every space calm, safe, and free of judgment so everyone can enjoy the music.

Love

We look out for each other, share water, and check in on friends and strangers alike.

Unity

No matter your background or favorite genre, the dance floor brings us together.

Respect

We respect each other's boundaries, the artists, the venues, and the planet.

Rave Safe

Taking care of yourself is part of PLUR. Stay hydrated, take breaks, protect your hearing, and always have a plan to get home. Learn more about festival safety from DanceSafe.
